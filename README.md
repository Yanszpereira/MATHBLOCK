## ✨ Overview

> **MATHBLOCK** is a 3D educational game developed as a high school final project (TCC), created to reinforce fundamental mathematics skills and encourage logical reasoning among elementary school students.

The project emerged from our observations of students struggling with basic mathematics and often lacking interest in traditional learning approaches.

Rather than replacing teachers or conventional education, **MATHBLOCK** aims to complement classroom learning by integrating mathematical reasoning directly into gameplay.

Set in a dreamlike world, players wield a **magical pencil** capable of manipulating numbered blocks and applying mathematical operations. These blocks become essential tools for solving puzzles, interacting with the environment, and progressing through the game.

## 🧠 Gameplay

The core gameplay combines **exploration, platforming, and mathematical problem-solving**.

Players manipulate numbered blocks using four fundamental operations:

- ➕ Addition
- ➖ Subtraction
- ✖️ Multiplication
- ➗ Division

> [!NOTE]
> The game does not use blocks with zero or negative values.

In addition to mathematical challenges, players must navigate:

- obstacles
- moving platforms
- doors
- time-based challenges

---

## 🗺️ Game Stages

### 1️⃣ Stage 1 — Fundamentals

Introduces movement, jumping, the magical pencil, numbered blocks, and the four basic operations.  
Players apply these mechanics to solve puzzles and pass through three doors.

### 2️⃣ Stage 2 — Scaling Blocks

Introduces blocks that change their physical size according to their numerical value.  
Players must manipulate these blocks to overcome environmental obstacles and progress.

### 3️⃣ Stage 3 — Equation Balance

Introduces the concept of equality through large interactive balancing scales.  
Players must understand and apply mathematical equivalence to balance the structures and unlock new paths.

### 4️⃣ Stage 4 — Final Challenge

Combines the previously introduced mechanics in a final challenge.  
Players solve mathematical expressions and navigate an arena featuring obstacles, time pressure, and challenges that test their accumulated skills.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Stage 4 is currently under development.

## 🧪 Testing & Feedback

MATHBLOCK has undergone **informal playtesting** with students from our institution and members of its intended audience.

During these sessions, team members:

- observed how participants interacted with the game
- asked questions about their experiences
- recorded feedback

These observations help guide improvements to gameplay, accessibility, and the clarity of mathematical challenges.

> [!CAUTION]
> The testing process is exploratory and does not constitute a formal evaluation of educational effectiveness.

## ▶️ Running the Project

MATHBLOCK is developed using **Unity 6000.3.9f1**.

To explore the project locally:

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open **Unity Hub**.
3. Add the `ProjetoMATHBLOCK` directory as an existing project.
4. Open it using **Unity Editor 6000.3.9f1**.
5. Wait for Unity to import the required assets and packages.
6. Open the main menu scene and enter **Play Mode**.

> [!TIP]
> Some features or development stages may require additional configuration.
